# Technical demonstration recording outline

This is a recording guide, not a completed video. Target 4–6 minutes. Narrate in English and keep the terminal and evidence text large enough to read. Record after the second live run and genuine student review; do not claim those steps have happened until they have.

| Time | Show | Explain |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:40 | SUBMISSION.md diagram and run.sh | Research Loop serves students deciding which experiments to delegate to agents. Bash coordinates independent collectors, extraction, storage, editorial stages and review. |
| 0:40–1:25 | One feed record, one video transcript, one podcast transcript | Source publication time differs from collection time. Show the actual UTC date window. Original-page recovery is supplemental web evidence and does not silently fulfill the RSS quota. |
| 1:25–2:25 | A published claim, its brief, decision and saved source passage | Trace a real claim_id to an exact item/version and quote or segment. Play the cited audio passage. Distinguish the author's claim from the series' inference. |
| 2:25–3:15 | Both live run summaries and changes.jsonl | Show two different actual UTC dates. Explain new, changed and unchanged records. Show an unchanged replay and why it is not the second live run. |
| 3:15–3:50 | Collector error receipt and recovery evidence | Explain the actual arXiv/subtitle collection failures, partial results and explicit coverage reporting. Do not describe failure as a successful empty search. |
| 3:50–4:40 | Your review worksheet and before/after policy outputs | Explain your own correction or disagreement, why the policy changed and how the rerun changed a decision or argument. Read your own filled-in analysis rather than an AI-generated approval. |
| 4:40–5:20 | review.json, approval.json and package manifest | Show the reviewed fingerprint. Explain why editing a draft invalidates old approval. Packaging prepares assets; Google Vids renders videos and platforms host the final publications. |
| 5:20–5:50 | Source versions and component boundaries | Discuss the tradeoff: retaining explicit source versions and intermediate artifacts adds work but makes claims inspectable. Local transcription avoids per-minute API billing but consumes machine time and still needs listening checks. |

Completion checklist: actual second live run; real student review and policy revision; final publication URLs; screen and narration recorded; video exported and link verified. All currently unfinished steps must remain labeled pending in the submission.
