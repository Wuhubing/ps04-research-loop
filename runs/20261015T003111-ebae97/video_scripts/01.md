### Scene 4 — Clear boundaries, limited comparisons

<!-- 01-c4 -->

**Narration**

The solar-panel segmentation study makes the boundaries explicit. Agents may edit the training program, but cannot edit the evaluator or train on validation or test images. Each campaign allows twenty-four logged experiments, including the baseline.

The limitations matter just as much. Every proposed change uses one random seed. Loops A and C have no test evaluation. Coding model and GPU class vary together. Our inference is that these results cannot identify a generally superior coding model or training recipe.

### Scene 6 — Running code versus meeting a claim
<!-- 01-c6 -->

**Narration**

ResearchFlow-Eval reports that only fourteen of ninety end-to-end claims met their criteria, including just one empirical claim. But this is not a universal success rate for research agents. The study uses an LLM judge, and its rubric follows an anchor paper. The authors acknowledge that this may undervalue scientifically valid alternatives. Read the result as evidence about this evaluation, with those limits attached.
