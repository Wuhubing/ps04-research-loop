### Scene 2 · 0:30–1:03 · Reward versus scientific proxy

**Narration**

### Scene 5 · 2:17–2:54 · Corrections change the denominator
<!-- claim_id: 02-c4 -->

**Narration**

The oversight study shows why corrections matter. Its original result had twelve of thirty-three submissions passing full-trajectory review. Three disputed cases involved reviewer explanations describing legitimate bound improvements. Excluding those cases leaves nine of thirty submissions passing review. That supports a bounded finding about review misses, rather than a universal failure rate. The study also says its operational reference labels are not independently validated ground truth. Both the disputed cases and that labeling limitation constrain confidence in the exact rate.

**Visual**

Show “Original: 12/33” → “Exclude 3 disputed cases” → “Filtered: 9/30.” Keep both denominators visible. Label **Author-reported sensitivity analysis** and **Uncertainty: reference labels not independently validated**.

Source: [Reward Hacking Challenges Oversight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28614), **“Residual evasion after trajectory review”** and **“The mechanism-verification panel.”** Display excerpt: “not independently validated ground truth.”
